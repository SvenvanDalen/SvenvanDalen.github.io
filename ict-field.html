The ICT field of work

23 October 2026

ICT is not only found at software companies. I see it every week at work.

The company: Paree

After I stopped my study at the TU, I applied for a job at Paree. That is where I got my first real programming puzzle, and I still work there part-time. Paree is an electrical engineering company that works in industrial automation: the control systems that make machines and installations in industry do their job. That is where electrical engineering and software engineering meet, and Paree does both.

Where software fits in

Next to the electrical engineers there is a small software team of a few people. I am part of it. My work is internal automation in C#: software that takes repetitive work off my colleagues' hands, so they can spend their time on the actual projects.

  • The electrical engineering and the software are part of the same projects
  • The software team is small, so everyone works on many different things
  • The people who use my software are my own colleagues

What it means for me

Working at Paree confirms the direction I want to go in. A small team where communication is direct, where what I build is actually used, and where several connected projects share one goal: that is the kind of place where I want to work as a software engineer after I graduate.
