Why IT?

I love taking something messy and structuring it until it makes sense.

Then · After the TU

After I stopped my study at the TU, I started working at Paree, an electrical engineering company. Soon after, they handed me a logical problem they'd been stuck on. I remember the frustration of not seeing the solution at first, and then the moment it started to click — piece by piece, until the problem simply disappeared. That feeling stayed with me. It's why I chose ICT: I love taking something messy and structuring it until it makes sense, in a way that's easy to build on afterward.

Now · HBO-ICT

Now, studying HBO-ICT, that same drive pushes me further. I don't just want code that works — I want to understand why certain architectural choices make a codebase easy to extend and maintain, so I study existing projects and ask for feedback on my own decisions. I also want to truly understand the people I build for, asking sharper questions before I start, so the solution actually fits the problem.

Next · The future

Eventually, I want to be the kind of engineer who uses the newest tools to build things that make people's lives easier — the same feeling I had with that first puzzle, just on a bigger scale.
