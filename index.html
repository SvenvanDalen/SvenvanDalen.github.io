Why IT is the right fit for me

I am Sven, a first-year IT student from Arnemuiden. I like solving problems, I don't like having them... And the solving of small problems or getting solutions, is exactly what building software feels like to me. If you want a bit more about me, I put that on a separate page.

The second reason is that IT never stands still. New languages, tools and ideas show up every year, and what counts as "best practice" keeps moving. Some people find that exhausting; I find it motivating. I would rather be in a field where I have to keep learning than one where I finish learning and then just repeat myself. Being comfortable with constant change is one of my strengths, and IT rewards it.

What makes IT click for me

  • Breaking a big problem into small, logical steps
  • The feeling when a stubborn bug finally makes sense
  • Building something real that other people can actually use
  • A field that keeps changing, so there is always more to learn
  • Clear feedback: the code either works or it doesn't
