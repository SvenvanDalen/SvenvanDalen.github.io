My Personal SWOT

Strengths

  • I don't give up easily.
  • When I get a new project, I do a lot of work at the start, so the end is less stressful.

Weaknesses

  • I'm not a planner, I just start with the project.
  • I don't like to speak in front of groups.

Opportunities

  • At my job at Paree, I program and develop software.
  • My experience at work can help me in group projects, because I already know how those projects work in real software projects.

Threats

  • I have a part-time job which takes some time every week.
  • Delays in group assignments caused by other team members.

My SMART Goals

Goal 1 · Strength

I will use my habit of starting early to finish my next school project without stress at the end.

Specific/Measurable: In the first week, I will complete the first part of the work and make a short plan for the rest.

Time-bound: I will have the full project finished at least 2 days before the deadline.

Goal 2 · Weakness

I will practice speaking in front of groups, so I feel more confident during presentations.

Specific/Measurable: I will volunteer to present at least one part of every group presentation, and practice it at home beforehand.

Time-bound: I will do this for all group presentations this semester, until 31 January 2027.
