Direction

14 October 2026

My first goal is to graduate. After that, this is where I want to go.

The team

I want to join a software engineering company with a small, friendly team, where communication is direct and everyone has real influence on the product.

The work

I would like to design backends and APIs using object-oriented programming, because I enjoy building systems that stay clear and maintainable.

The projects

Working on several connected projects with one main goal would give me variety in the problems I solve, while keeping the bigger picture in sight.

The tools

I want to work with the latest tools, as they make engineering faster, more reliable, and more enjoyable, and they keep me learning.
